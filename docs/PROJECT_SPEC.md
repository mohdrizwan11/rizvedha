# SSC CGL GK — Project Spec & Syllabus Coverage Reference

Save this file in the repository as `/docs/PROJECT_SPEC.md`. Re-read it at the start of every session. Current Affairs is EXCLUDED from this project.

## Standing rules (apply to every batch)

1. Never invent facts, PYQs, exam years, shifts or frequency statistics. Unverifiable items are labelled "unverified".
2. A fact is "verified" only with two independent, fetched sources. Never cite a page that could not be fetched.
3. Verified SSC PYQs, original practice questions and pattern-inspired questions are always labelled separately.
4. No paywall bypass, no copying of proprietary notes, question banks or images. All text, diagrams and questions are original.
5. Every image has a licence record (original SVG, public domain, or CC licence with attribution).
6. Maps are SVG (d3-geo + React). No MapLibre/WebGL/PostGIS. Boundaries must follow India's official depiction.
7. Time-sensitive facts carry a `last_verified` date.
8. Preserve existing user progress. One canonical mistakes store.
9. After every batch: stop and report counts, sources (and failures), unverified list, honest PYQ count, tests, bundle size, what is broken.

## Syllabus coverage reference (SSC does not publish a chapter-wise GA syllabus; this is a practical expansion)

### 1. History

**Ancient:** Prehistoric (Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic, Chalcolithic); Indus Valley (Harappa, Mohenjo-Daro, Lothal, Dholavira, town planning, seals, trade, decline); Vedic period (Early/Later, Vedas, Upanishads, Brahmanas, Sabha, Samiti); Mahajanapadas, Magadha (Haryanka, Shishunaga, Nanda); Jainism & Buddhism (founders, councils, texts, sects); Mauryan (Chandragupta, Bindusara, Ashoka, Kalinga, Arthashastra); Post-Mauryan (Shungas, Kanvas, Indo-Greeks, Shakas, Kushanas, Kanishka); Gupta (Chandragupta I, Samudragupta, Chandragupta II, administration, literature, science, art); Post-Gupta (Harsha, Pallavas, Chalukyas); South Indian kingdoms (Cholas, Cheras, Pandyas, Rashtrakutas); ancient literature (Ramayana, Mahabharata, Sangam, Puranas); ancient science (Aryabhata, Varahamihira, Brahmagupta, Charaka, Sushruta); ancient art & architecture (stupas, pillars, caves, temples). **Medieval:** Rajputs, Palas, Pratiharas, Rashtrakutas; Delhi Sultanate (Slave, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi); sultanate administration (iqta, military, taxation, markets); important rulers (Aibak, Iltutmish, Razia, Alauddin Khilji, Muhammad bin Tughlaq, Firoz Shah); Mughals (Babur to Aurangzeb); Mughal administration (mansabdari, jagirdari, revenue); Mughal architecture; regional kingdoms (Vijayanagara, Bahmani, Marathas, Ahom, Sikh); Bhakti; Sufi; medieval literature; travellers (Al-Biruni, Ibn Battuta, Marco Polo, Bernier). **Modern:** Arrival of Europeans; British expansion (Carnatic Wars, Plassey, Buxar); Governor-Generals; policies (Subsidiary Alliance, Doctrine of Lapse, Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, Mahalwari); Acts (1773 to 1935); Revolt of 1857; socio-religious reforms & reformers; tribal & peasant movements; INC formation, Moderates/Extremists; Partition of Bengal & Swadeshi; revolutionary movement; Muslim League; Gandhian era (Champaran, Kheda, Ahmedabad, NCM, CDM, Quit India); events (Jallianwala, Simon, Nehru Report, Round Tables); constitutional development; independence (INA, Naval Mutiny, Partition). **Chronology to memorise:** 1885, 1905, 1906, 1907, 1916, 1917, 1919, 1920, 1930, 1931, 1942, 1947. World history: selective high-yield basics only (revolutions, world wars).

### 2. Geography

**Physical:** universe & solar system; earth (shape, rotation, revolution, seasons, time zones); latitudes & longitudes; earth's interior; rocks; landforms; plate tectonics; atmosphere; winds; rainfall; climate (monsoon, El Nino, La Nina); oceanography (floors, currents, tides, waves); natural disasters. **Indian:** location & neighbours; physiographic divisions; mountain ranges; rivers & basins; lakes; dams; climate & monsoon; soils; natural vegetation; agriculture (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid); minerals; industries; transport; population & census; states & geography. **World:** continents, oceans, mountains, plateaus, deserts, rivers, lakes, straits & canals, grasslands, countries/capitals/currencies, important locations, natural resources. **Economic geography:** minerals, industrial & agricultural regions, energy, trade routes, ports, population distribution, transport networks.

### 3. Indian Polity & Constitution

**Foundation:** historical background (1773–1947); Constituent Assembly; constitution making; salient features; Preamble; sources; parts; articles; 12 schedules; amendments; citizenship (Arts 5–11). **Rights & duties:** Fundamental Rights (Arts 12–35: equality 14–18, freedom 19–22, exploitation 23–24, religion 25–28, cultural/educational 29–30, remedies 32 and writs); DPSP (36–51); Fundamental Duties (51A); emergency provisions. **Union:** President, Vice-President, PM, Council of Ministers, Parliament, procedures (question hour, zero hour), bills, committees (PAC, Estimates, COPU), Speaker, Attorney General. **State & local:** Governor, CM, State Legislature, High Courts, Panchayati Raj (73rd), Municipalities (74th), Gram Sabha. **Judiciary & bodies:** Supreme Court, High Courts, judicial review, PIL, Election Commission, UPSC, Finance Commission, CAG, national commissions, NITI Aayog, NHRC, CVC, CIC, Lokpal; constitutional vs statutory vs executive bodies. **Concepts:** federalism & Centre-State relations; Inter-State Council; anti-defection; official language; amendment procedure; basic structure; writs; schedules & amendments; landmark judgments.

### 4. Economics

**Basic:** scope, systems (capitalism/socialism/mixed), sectors, demand, supply, markets, utility, production, cost, revenue, national income (GDP, GNP, NNP, NDP), growth vs development. **Indian economy:** structure, planning/Five-Year Plans, NITI Aayog, agricultural revolutions (Green, White, Blue, Yellow...), industry (MSME, Make in India), services, poverty, unemployment, inflation (CPI, WPI), fiscal policy, monetary policy (repo, reverse repo, CRR, SLR), budget & deficits, taxation (direct, indirect, GST), external sector, WTO/IMF/World Bank. **Banking:** RBI, commercial banks, terms (NPA etc.), NABARD/SIDBI/EXIM, UPI/NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, financial inclusion, capital market, NSE/BSE/SEBI, insurance/IRDAI, instruments. **Schemes (static facts only):** Jan Dhan, MGNREGA, PM-KISAN, Fasal Bima, Ayushman Bharat, Mudra, Startup India, Stand-Up India, Skill India, NFSA, PDS, DBT. Seven points each: launch year, ministry, objective, beneficiaries, features, funding, updates. **Organisations & indices:** IMF, World Bank, WTO, ADB, AIIB, UNDP, UNCTAD, ILO; HDI, Global Hunger Index, Global Innovation Index.

### 5. General Science

**Physics:** units & measurement, motion, laws of motion, force & gravitation, work & energy, heat, sound, light, human eye, electricity, magnetism, modern physics, optics, devices. **Chemistry:** matter, atomic structure, periodic table, bonding, reactions, acids/bases/salts, metals & non-metals, carbon chemistry, everyday chemistry, gases, fuels, environmental chemistry. **Biology:** cell, tissues, human systems (digestive, respiratory, circulatory, excretory, nervous, endocrine, reproductive), genetics, evolution, nutrition & deficiency diseases, diseases, immunity, blood, plant biology, classification, ecology. **Sci-tech:** space (ISRO, missions), defence/DRDO, nuclear, biotech, IT, medical research, Nobel discoveries, scientific institutions.

### 6. Static GK & Culture

Art & culture: classical dances, folk dances, music, instruments, paintings, architecture (Nagara, Dravida, Vesara), temples, caves, UNESCO sites, festivals, handicrafts, theatre. Books & literature. Awards (civilian, gallantry, literary, film, sports, international, scientific). Sports & games. Organisations (UN & agencies, financial, regional, security, Indian institutions, regulators). Miscellaneous (symbols, parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves, dams, stadiums, airports/seaports, firsts, largest/smallest, days & themes, states & capitals, UTs, HQs, currencies).

### 7. Environment & Ecology

Ecology (ecosystem, food chain/web, pyramids, biogeochemical cycles, succession, interactions, biomes); biodiversity & conservation (hotspots, endemic/endangered species, IUCN, Wildlife Act 1972, Biological Diversity Act 2002, Project Tiger/Elephant/Snow Leopard, national parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves, Ramsar); pollution (air, water, soil, noise, plastic, e-waste, ozone, greenhouse, global warming, waste); climate change & conventions (UNFCCC, Kyoto, Paris, Montreal, CBD, CITES, Ramsar, IPCC, COP, SDGs, International Solar Alliance).
