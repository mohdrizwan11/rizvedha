🧠 Quick Mnemonics for Classical Dances

🔑 "B-KOK-MOS" — 8 Classical Dances
Bharatanatyam (TN) · Kathak (UP) · Odissi (Odisha) · Kuchipudi (AP) · Mohiniyattam (Kerala) · O... wait—
Better: "BKKOMMS" = Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Odissi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, Sattriya (+ Kuchipudi)
🔑 Kerala has 2 Classical Dances: K+M
Kathakali = King's drama (elaborate, male, Ramayana)
Mohiniyattam = Mohini (feminine, enchanting)
🔑 Sattriya = Assam = Shankaradeva = 2000
"S-A-S-2000" — Sattriya, Assam, Shankaradeva, Year 2000 recognition

🔥 TOP 20 PATTERN OBSERVATIONS — SSC CGL Static GK

  1. Books & Authors: Autobiographies of freedom fighters asked almost every year — Gandhi, Nehru, Bose, Ambedkar.
  2. Important Days: "Who/what event caused this day?" type questions — Feb 28 (Raman Effect), Sep 5 (Radhakrishnan), Nov 14 (Nehru).
  3. Classical Dances: "Which dance belongs to which state?" + Odissi vs Odisha distinction. Sattriya (Assam) is a hot pick.
  4. UNESCO World Heritage: Correct year/location pairings — Ajanta (1983), Konark (1984), Khajuraho (1986), Fatehpur Sikri (1986).
  5. Bharat Ratna: First recipient, first woman, first sportsperson, first non-Indian are 4 direct Q-types.
  6. Nobel Prize: Indian/Indian-origin winners — Tagore 1913, Raman 1930, Mother Teresa 1979, Amartya Sen 1998, Kailash Satyarthi 2014.
  7. Organization HQ: WHO-Geneva, FAO-Rome, UNESCO-Paris, UNICEF-New York — these 4 asked every alternate year.
  8. Rivers: Longest peninsular river = Godavari, "Dakshin Ganga" = Godavari (same answer!)
  9. Mountains: Highest peak in India = K2 (not Everest!), Highest in South India = Anamudi.
  10. National Symbols: "Which is NOT a national symbol?" type negative questions. Hockey is NOT officially declared.
  11. ISRO: Chandrayaan-3 (2023 South Pole landing), Mangalyaan (first attempt success), Aditya-L1 are very hot topics now.
  12. Tricky Capitals: UAE=Abu Dhabi, Turkey=Ankara, Switzerland=Bern asked repeatedly. NEVER Delhi for India type question.
  13. Polity — FRs: Article 21 (Life & Liberty), 21A (Education), 32 (Constitutional Remedies) are in almost every paper.
  14. Polity — Amendments: 42nd (Socialist+Secular+Integrity), 44th (Property), 61st (Voting age 18), 86th (RTE) are gold.
  15. Writs: Habeas Corpus definition + use asked repeatedly. "Which writ for illegal detention?" = Habeas Corpus.
  16. Schedules: 8th Schedule (languages), 10th Schedule (anti-defection), 7th Schedule (3 lists) — all hot.
  17. Ancient Books: Arthashastra=Kautilya, Indica=Megasthenes, Abhijnanasakuntalam=Kalidasa — foundation questions.
  18. Instruments: "Which instrument measures X?" — Barometer (pressure), Seismograph (earthquake), Lactometer (milk purity).
  19. Firsts in India: First to climb Everest (Tenzing+Edmund), First woman Everest (Bachendri Pal), First Indian space (Rakesh Sharma).
  20. Emergency: Art 356 (President's Rule) "most misused" + Art 352 history (1975 Emergency) — conceptual questions asked.
