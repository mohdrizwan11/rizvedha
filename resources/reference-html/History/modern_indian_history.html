British Expansion in India

1757–1857 · Policies, Wars & Conquest
📌TWO KEY POLICIES
SUBSIDIARY ALLIANCE (Lord Wellesley, 1798)

Indian rulers must:
• Maintain British army in their territory
• Pay for army (or cede territory)
• Accept a British Resident at court
• Give up independent foreign relations

First adopted by: Nizam of Hyderabad (1798)
DOCTRINE OF LAPSE (Lord Dalhousie, 1848)

If an Indian ruler died without a natural male heir, territory ANNEXED

Annexed: Satara (1848), Jhansi (1853), Nagpur (1853), Awadh (1856 — misgovernance)

Effect: Major cause of 1857 revolt
⚔️MAJOR WARS OF EXPANSION
1766–99
Anglo-Mysore Wars (4 Wars)
Hyder Ali vs British in 1st & 2nd wars. Tipu Sultan (Tiger of Mysore) in 3rd & 4th. 4th War (1799): Tipu Sultan died defending Srirangapatnam. Mysore restored to Wadiyar dynasty.
Tipu = Tiger of Mysore4th War → 1799 Tipu dies
1775–1818
Anglo-Maratha Wars (3 Wars)
1st (1775–82): Treaty of Salbai — status quo. 2nd (1803–05): Wellesley defeated Scindia, Bhonsle. 3rd (1817–18): Hastings — Peshwa Bajirao II surrendered; Maratha confederacy dissolved.
3rd War → End of Maratha power
1814–16
Anglo-Nepalese (Gurkha) War
Treaty of Sagauli (1816) — Nepal ceded Sikkim, Kumaon, Garhwal. British got Simla hill station. Gurkha soldiers began serving in British army.
1843
Conquest of Sindh
Charles Napier conquered Sindh. Famous quote: "Peccavi" (I have sinned/Sindh). Baluchi Amirs defeated.
1845–49
Anglo-Sikh Wars (2 Wars)
1st (1845–46): Treaty of Lahore. 2nd (1848–49): Dalhousie — Punjab ANNEXED 1849. Koh-i-Noor diamond taken to Britain.
Punjab annexed 1849Koh-i-Noor → England

The Revolt of 1857

First War of Independence · The Sepoy Mutiny
⚡ MOST IMPORTANT TOPIC — Avg 3–5 Questions per SSC exam
Started: 10 May 1857, Meerut | Ended: 1858 | Governor-General: Lord Canning
🔥CAUSES OF 1857 REVOLT
🏛️
POLITICAL CAUSES
  • Doctrine of Lapse — annexation of states
  • Awadh annexed 1856 — angered sepoys
  • Abolition of Mughal titles
  • Displacement of Indian princes
💼
ECONOMIC CAUSES
  • Exploitation of Indian economy
  • Heavy taxation on peasants
  • Destruction of Indian handicrafts
  • Free trade policy ruined merchants
⚙️
MILITARY CAUSES
  • Indian sepoys paid less than British
  • General Service Enlistment Act 1856
  • Racial discrimination in promotions
  • Greased cartridges — cow & pig fat
🕌
RELIGIOUS/SOCIAL CAUSES
  • Fear of religious conversion by missionaries
  • Social reforms seen as interference
  • Rumour — bone dust in flour
  • Western education threatening tradition
🗡️SPARK & IMMEDIATE CAUSE
Enfield Rifle Cartridge: Greased with cow fat (offensive to Hindus) and pig fat (offensive to Muslims).
Mangal Pandey (29th BNI, Barrackpore) fired at British officers 29 March 1857 — first rebel. Hanged 8 April 1857.
Full Revolt: 10 May 1857 at Meerut → marched to Delhi, proclaimed Bahadur Shah II emperor.

Socio-Religious Reform Movements

19th Century Renaissance · Reformers & Organizations
🌺 BRAHMO SAMAJ (1828) — Raja Ram Mohan Roy▼
Founded: 1828 by Raja Ram Mohan Roy — "Father of Indian Renaissance" / "Father of Modern India"
Key beliefs: Monotheism, rationalism, opposed idol worship, supported widow remarriage, women's education
Achievement: Abolition of Sati (1829) — Lord William Bentinck passed Bengal Sati Regulation Act
Newspapers: Sambad Kaumudi (Bengali), Mirat-ul-Akhbar (Persian)
Later: Keshab Chandra Sen split → Brahmo Samaj of India (1866) and Adi Brahmo Samaj
⚡ Most Asked: Sati abolition 1829
☀️ ARYA SAMAJ (1875) — Swami Dayanand Saraswati▼
Founded: 1875 in Bombay | Motto: "Back to the Vedas"
Beliefs: Vedic authority, opposed idol worship and caste by birth; supported women education & widow remarriage
Book: Satyarth Prakash (Light of Truth)
Shuddhi Movement — reconversion of Hindus
DAV Schools: Founded after Dayanand's death
Born: 1824, Tankara, Gujarat; Died: 1883, Ajmer
⚡ "Back to Vedas" = Dayanand
🕉️ RAMAKRISHNA MISSION (1897) — Swami Vivekananda▼
Ramakrishna Paramahamsa (1836–1886): Mystic saint; Guru of Vivekananda; worshipped at Dakshineswar temple
Swami Vivekananda (born Narendranath Datta, 1863–1902):
• Founded Ramakrishna Mission (1897)
• Famous Chicago Speech at Parliament of World Religions — 11 Sep 1893
• Preached Vedanta — "Service to man = service to God"
• Quote: "Arise, Awake and Stop not till the goal is reached"
Chicago Speech → 1893 Ramakrishna Mission → 1897
🧠 THEOSOPHICAL SOCIETY (1875) — Annie Besant▼
Founded: 1875 in New York by Madame H.P. Blavatsky and Col. Henry Steel Olcott
India HQ: Adyar, Madras (1882)
Annie Besant became president 1907 — founded Central Hindu College, Banaras (1898); Congress President 1917 (1st woman president)
Home Rule League — Besant founded Sep 1916
Annie Besant → Home Rule League 1916 → Congress President 1917

Indian National Movement

1885–1920 · INC · Moderates · Extremists · Swadeshi
⚡ INC Founded: 28 December 1885
Founder: A.O. Hume (retired ICS officer) | First Session: Bombay, Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College
First President: W.C. Bonnerjee | 72 delegates attended | Viceroy: Lord Dufferin
⚖️MODERATES vs EXTREMISTS
FeatureModerates (1885–1905)Extremists (1905+)
MethodConstitutional, petitions, prayersPassive resistance, swadeshi, boycott
GoalSelf-governance within British EmpireSwaraj (complete self-rule)
LeadersDadabhai Naoroji, Gokhale, Pherozeshah Mehta, Surendranath BanerjeeBal Gangadhar Tilak, Lal Lajpat Rai, Bipin Chandra Pal (Lal-Bal-Pal)
Key theoryDrain of Wealth (Naoroji)"Swaraj is my birthright" (Tilak)
✂️PARTITION OF BENGAL 1905 — TURNING POINT
Lord Curzon partitioned Bengal on 16 October 1905
Real reason: Divide Hindu-Muslim Bengal; weaken nationalism
Response: Swadeshi Movement + Boycott Movement launched
16 Oct 1905 = day of mourning; people tied rakhi across religious lines
Partition annulled: 1911 by Lord Hardinge at Delhi Durbar (Delhi became capital)
🔥 SWADESHI MOVEMENT
  • Use Indian-made goods
  • Bonfire of foreign cloth
  • Promoted Indian cottage industries
  • Students, women, peasants participated
  • Tagore composed Amar Sonar Bangla
💡 SURAT SPLIT 1907
  • INC split at Surat session
  • Moderates vs Extremists clash
  • Tilak expelled from Congress
  • Reunited at Lucknow 1916
  • Lucknow Pact 1916 — INC + Muslim League
Home Rule Movement (1916):
Tilak's League (Apr 1916) + Annie Besant's League (Sep 1916)
Goal: Self-government within British Empire
Besant became 1st woman INC President (Calcutta, 1917)

Gandhi Era & Mass Movements

1919–1947 · NCM · CDM · Quit India · Independence
Mahatma Gandhi: Born 2 Oct 1869, Porbandar | Returned from South Africa: 9 Jan 1915
Weapons: Satyagraha + Non-Cooperation + Civil Disobedience
🌾GANDHI'S EARLY MOVEMENTS
1917 — Champaran Satyagraha
Bihar — against Indigo planters' Tinkathia system. Gandhi's first Satyagraha in India. First Civil Disobedience in India by Gandhi.
1918 — Kheda + Ahmedabad
Kheda (Gujarat) — against tax despite crop failure. Ahmedabad — 1st hunger strike by Gandhi; supported mill workers.
1919 — Rowlatt Act & Jallianwala Bagh
Rowlatt Act (Black Act): Imprisonment without trial; Gandhi called hartal 6 April 1919.
Jallianwala Bagh: 13 April 1919 (Baisakhi) — Gen. Dyer fired at unarmed crowd in Amritsar. 1000+ killed. Tagore renounced knighthood.
Jallianwala → 13 Apr 1919Tagore → returned knighthood
NON-COOPERATION MOVEMENT (NCM) 1920–22:
Launched: 1 August 1920 | Reasons: Jallianwala Bagh + Khilafat issue
Programme: Return titles, boycott schools/courts/councils, boycott foreign goods
Khilafat Movement: Ali Brothers (Muhammad Ali, Shaukat Ali)
Suspended: Feb 1922 after Chauri Chaura incident (22 Feb 1922) — mob burnt police station, 22 policemen killed
CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE MOVEMENT (CDM) 1930–34:
Lahore session 31 Dec 1929 → Nehru presided → Purna Swaraj declared
Dandi March: Gandhi walked 241 miles from Sabarmati to Dandi — 12 March to 6 April 1930
Gandhi-Irwin Pact: 5 March 1931 — CDM suspended
Poona Pact 1932: Gandhi + Ambedkar — reserved seats for depressed classes
QUIT INDIA MOVEMENT (1942):
Launched: 8 August 1942, Bombay (Gowalia Tank Maidan)
Slogan: "Do or Die" | Also called: August Kranti
Reason: Failure of Cripps Mission 1942 ("post-dated cheque on a crashing bank")
Aruna Asaf Ali hoisted Congress flag at Gowalia Tank
Parallel govts: Tamluk, Satara, Ballia
SUBHAS CHANDRA BOSE (Netaji, b. 23 Jan 1897):
INC President 1938 (Haripura) & 1939 (Tripuri) → resigned; founded Forward Bloc 1939
INA/Azad Hind Fauj — organized in Singapore 1942 (Mohan Singh → Rash Behari → Netaji)
Slogan: "Jai Hind" and "Give me blood, I will give you freedom"
Azad Hind Govt proclaimed Singapore 1943 | Died: Taipei, 18 Aug 1945
⚡ GANDHI TIMELINE
1917 Champaran → 1919 Jallianwala → 1920-22 NCM → 1922 Chauri Chaura → 1930 Dandi → 1931 Gandhi-Irwin → 1942 Quit India → 1947 Independence
