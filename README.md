# Riz Vedha

**The whole SSC CGL General Studies syllabus as pictures, cards and drills.**

121 chapters · 15,900+ book facts · 33 Explore libraries · 12 hand-drawn
diagrams · 741 practice questions · 4 games.

A visual GS library built from the **Parmar SSC Fatman GK/GS** theory book
(English, 2nd edition), with respectful thanks to Parmar Sir — his book is the
source of every chapter, fact and page reference here.

---

## What you can do

- **Learn** the full static-GS syllabus, chapter by chapter, with readable
  sections, flashcards and practice questions.
- **Explore** 33 topic libraries — rivers, national parks, dances, temples,
  monuments, railways, space, sports and more — as lists, maps and quizzes.
- **See it** in 12 hand-drawn diagrams: the solar system, the water cycle, the
  Constitution, the cell, and other ideas that are easier to remember as
  pictures.
- **Play** Rapid Fire, Fact or Bluff, Match and Sprint.
- **Track** your streak, XP, chapters finished, subject mastery and a 53-week
  activity grid.
- **Save** any fact or card for later.

## Using it

Open the site and start. Nothing to install, no account required.

Your progress, streaks, bookmarks and game bests are saved in your browser
automatically. Nothing leaves your device.

This is static GS only. There is no Current Affairs section, by design.
